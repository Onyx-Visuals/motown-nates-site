Fresh Off the Board

Eat in the Teepee

Motown Nate's is a food truck built from a vintage silver camper, with an outdoor dining setup you won't find anywhere else in the Soo.

Teepee Seating

Dine inside the teepee around a guitar-shaped campfire stove. Cozy on chilly evenings, shaded in summer.

Picnic Tables

Plenty of outdoor tables under cover for sunny days, groups, and quick lunches.

Dogs Welcome

Water dishes come out for four-legged guests. Bring the whole pack.

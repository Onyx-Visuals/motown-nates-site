Catering

The same smoker, the same hand-cut fries, brought to your crew, your party, or your event.

What We Bring

Everything on the regular menu can be scaled up for a group. The most popular orders for a crowd:

  • Smoked brisket and pulled pork by the pan, with buns, pickles, red onion, and BBQ sauce
  • Taco bars with street, carnitas, or brisket tacos and all the fixings
  • BBQ loaded rice bowls and The Bad Hombre burritos
  • Sides including hand-cut fries, onion rings, mac and cheese, and coleslaw
  • Boxed lunches with a sandwich, side, and chips for office and job-site orders

Good For

  • Work lunches and job-site crews
  • Birthdays, graduations, and family reunions
  • Company picnics and team events
  • Tailgates and game days
  • Any group of 10 or more

How It Works

  1. Call or text 906-748-4892 with your date, headcount, and what you're thinking.
  2. We'll build a menu and quote that fits your group and budget.
  3. Pick up at 800 Ashmun St or ask about delivery in the Sault area.

Please give us at least 48 hours notice for orders over 20 people so the brisket has time to smoke.
